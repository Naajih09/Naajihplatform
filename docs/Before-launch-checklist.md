# September Launch Checklist

This tracks non-code launch work that sits outside the app repository.

## Domain And Secure Name

- Choose the final public domain, for example `naajihbiz.com`.
- Buy the domain through a reputable registrar and enable domain lock.
- Add DNS records for the user app, admin app, API, and transactional email.
- Configure HTTPS, production frontend URLs, API URLs, and certificate verification URLs after DNS is live.
- Protect brand handles by reserving matching social names where possible.

## Email Campaign

- Prepare a launch email sequence for approximately 1,000 contacts.
- Verify the sending domain before sending bulk email.
- Use segmented lists: founders, investors, academy learners, partners, and internal testers.
- Include unsubscribe handling and avoid sending from personal inboxes.
- Track delivery, opens, clicks, bounces, and replies.

## Waitlist Goal

- Target 500 to 1,000 waitlist users before public launch.
- Review waitlist quality weekly by role, location, and interest.
- Invite in batches so support and verification review do not get overwhelmed.
- Convert waitlist entries into accounts with a clear onboarding path.

## Partners And Investment

- Build a shortlist of grant partners, angel networks, accelerators, universities, and ethical finance groups.
- Prepare a one-page partner memo with mission, traction, user roles, and launch target.
- Prepare an investor memo with market, product, revenue model, risks, and next milestones.
- Track outreach status, next follow-up date, decision maker, and requested materials.

## Skills And Staffing

- Identify people willing to contribute skills before launch: design, content, academy facilitation, verification review, sales, support, and community moderation.
- Start with part-time or volunteer contributors where possible, then convert reliable contributors into staff roles.
- Define ownership for launch-critical operations: email, waitlist review, partner outreach, support, and academy content.
